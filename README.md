### Out now

**Hold to Speed for YouTube**

Hold a key to speed up playback, double-click either side of the player to skip or rewind. The mobile long-press gesture, on your keyboard. Free, Manifest V3, collects nothing.

https://larkbrooks.dev/en/products/hold-to-speed/

### How these are built

Free, and staying free: no paid tier, no subscription, no in-app purchase, no ads.

Nothing leaves your browser: no accounts, no analytics, no network requests of their own.

One thing each. A tool that does one job well beats a suite nobody reads the settings of.
